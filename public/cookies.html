HedraFi uses cookies for authentication and session management so that essential account and access features can work as intended.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website may store on your browser or device. They can help a site remember a session and maintain essential functionality between page requests.

2. How HedraFi uses cookies

HedraFi uses cookies for authentication and session management. These cookies help the Services recognize an authenticated session, maintain access to protected features, and support basic security. They are not used by HedraFi for advertising or cross-site advertising tracking.

Our stated approach: authentication only. This policy should be updated if analytics, personalization, advertising, or other non-essential cookies are added in the future.

4. Managing cookies

You can usually view, block, or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential authentication cookies may prevent sign-in or cause some features to stop working correctly. Deleting cookies may sign you out and require you to authenticate again.

5. Third-party services

HedraFi does not use its own cookies for third-party advertising. If you choose to connect a wallet or open an external service, that service may operate under its own cookie and privacy practices. We do not control cookies set directly by external websites.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this Cookie Policy if the Services or our use of cookies changes. The latest version will be available on this page. For questions, contact HedraFi through the official channels linked from hedrafi.xyz.
