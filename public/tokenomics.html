03 / Utility philosophy

Utility should connect to real product experiences.

HedraFi's long-term approach is to develop token-related features around participation in the ecosystem, rather than framing the token as a promise of financial returns.

◈

Ecosystem participation

Support programs that encourage useful contributions and sustained involvement in the HedraFi ecosystem.

▦

Creator experiences

Develop marketplace and creator-facing features where token utility may support access to product functionality, subject to published terms.

◎

Long-term development

Keep resources available for infrastructure, community initiatives, and future product needs as the project evolves.

HDFI and HRT are different tokens. HRT has been used as a staking reward token. Do not assume HRT converts into HDFI or that the tokens share the same supply, utility, or distribution terms unless HedraFi explicitly announces it.
