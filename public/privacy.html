HedraFi · Privacy Policy

Privacy, with clarity.

We aim to collect only what is useful to operate the HedraFi experience, communicate with our users, and protect the integrity of our Services.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how HedraFi (“HedraFi,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit or use our website, NFT marketplace, creator storefronts, and related services (the “Services”). By using the Services, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

This page is a general template and should be reviewed for the actual systems, vendors, and legal requirements that apply to HedraFi before publication.

2. Information we handle

The information we handle depends on how you use the Services. It may include:

  • Contact information: such as an email address, if you voluntarily provide it to receive updates, contact support, or use a feature that requires it.
  • Wallet and transaction information: public wallet addresses, transaction identifiers, NFT ownership and transfer information, and other activity visible on Hedera or another supported blockchain.
  • Authentication and technical information: information necessary to maintain a signed-in session, protect accounts, prevent abuse, and keep the Services operating.
  • Messages you send us: information included in support requests or other communications.

Please do not send us passwords, private keys, seed phrases, or other wallet secrets. HedraFi will never need your seed phrase to provide marketplace functionality.

3. How we use information

We use information only for legitimate purposes connected with operating and improving the Services, including to:

  • authenticate users and maintain secure sessions;
  • provide marketplace and storefront functionality and display relevant on-chain information;
  • respond to questions, provide support, and communicate service-related information;
  • send product news or announcements to users who have requested or otherwise consented to receive them, where required;
  • detect fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our terms; and
  • meet applicable legal obligations and protect the rights, safety, and integrity of users and the Services.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information or share your email address with third parties for their own marketing. We use email information to communicate with users about HedraFi and its Services, subject to applicable law and your preferences.

We do not disclose personal information to third parties except where necessary to operate the Services at your direction, where you choose to connect an external service or wallet, where information is already publicly available on a blockchain, where disclosure is required by law, or where reasonably necessary to protect users, security, rights, or the Services. For example, network infrastructure, wallet software, or blockchain explorers that you choose to use may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control those independent services.

Before publishing, ensure this section accurately reflects any hosting, email delivery, wallet connection, security, or other service providers actually used by the website.

5. Wallets and public networks

Blockchain transactions are generally public, persistent, and difficult or impossible to delete or change. If you connect a wallet, submit a transaction, buy or list an NFT, or otherwise interact with a public network, your wallet address and transaction details may be visible to anyone and may be copied or indexed by independent services. These records are not private merely because you use HedraFi. We cannot remove data from a public blockchain.

Connecting a wallet does not necessarily reveal your legal identity to us. However, wallet activity may be linkable to you by you or others, depending on the information you disclose and other available data.

6. Cookies

HedraFi uses cookies for authentication and session management, such as helping the Services recognize an authenticated session and protect access. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising or cross-site advertising tracking. If this changes, we will update this policy and provide any notices or choices required by law.

For more details, see our Cookie Policy.

7. Security and retention

We use reasonable measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No website, wallet integration, network, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your wallet credentials and using a secure device.

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including to provide the Services, respond to requests, resolve disputes, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. Public blockchain data may remain permanently on the relevant network.

8. Your choices and rights

You may choose not to provide optional information such as an email address, though certain features or communications may then be unavailable. If you receive non-essential email updates, you may opt out using the unsubscribe method provided or by contacting us. Service and security messages may still be sent when necessary.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of certain personal information. These rights may be limited by applicable law and do not allow us to erase public blockchain records. Contact us to make a request.

9. Children

The Services are not intended for children who are not legally able to enter into the relevant transactions under the laws that apply to them. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can assess and address the issue.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as our Services or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies to future use, subject to applicable law.

For privacy questions or requests, contact HedraFi through the official contact channels linked from hedrafi.xyz.
