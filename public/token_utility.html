The Utility Layer of the HedraFi Marketplace

The official HedraFi token is designed as the utility asset for premium marketplace functionality, creator participation and future HedraFi ecosystem services. This document describes the token's intended utility, supply architecture, distribution and vesting structure.

1. Executive Overview

HedraFi's official token is being introduced as a utility asset directly connected to the HedraFi marketplace. Its purpose is to give creators and storefront owners a simple on-chain way to access premium marketplace functionality.

The initial utility architecture focuses on marketplace services rather than treating the token primarily as a speculative asset. The token can be acquired through the HedraFi Mainnet bonding-curve mechanism and is intended to be used inside the marketplace for selected premium features.

Utility principle: The token's initial economic purpose is tied to actual product functionality: users acquire the token and use it to access selected marketplace services. The documentation does not represent a guaranteed market price, investment return or future valuation.

3. How the Token Connects to the Marketplace

The intended user flow keeps the token's purpose straightforward: acquire the token, connect a wallet, select an eligible marketplace feature and authorize the required token payment. Once the marketplace confirms the payment, the selected feature can be activated according to its configured terms.

Acquire HDFI→ Connect Wallet→ Select Premium Feature→ Approve & Pay→ Feature Activated
Example: A creator who wants additional visibility can select an eligible collection, choose a featured-placement option, authorize the required HDFI payment and receive the corresponding marketplace service.

4. Mainnet Token Acquisition

HedraFi has deployed a Mainnet bonding-curve contract that provides a direct HBAR-to-HDFI acquisition path. The current curve is intentionally buy-only: it does not provide an on-contract HDFI redemption function.

Mainnet

Primary Acquisition

Users can purchase HDFI from the deployed curve by sending HBAR. The contract calculates the purchase cost from the configured curve parameters and transfers HDFI through the Hedera Token Service precompile.

Secondary Market

Future Liquidity

Secondary-market liquidity is a separate mechanism from the bonding curve. The bonding curve itself is not represented as a DEX liquidity pool or as a redemption facility.

The bonding curve's current design is one-directional. No claim is made here that users can sell HDFI back to the curve.

6. Initial Economic Reference

For initial economic modelling, HedraFi uses a reference value of $0.001 per HDFI.

At the maximum supply of 1,000,000,000 HDFI, that reference value corresponds to an illustrative fully diluted value of approximately $1,000,000. This figure is an internal economic reference for modelling and utility planning.

The reference value is not a guaranteed market price, valuation, yield or investment return. Actual market pricing, if and when secondary-market liquidity is established, may differ.

7. Staking Reward Migration & Vesting

HedraFi's earlier HBAR staking program distributed HRT to participating users. The transition toward the official token is designed to use a controlled vesting structure for eligible staking-related allocations rather than placing all eligible allocations into unrestricted circulation at once.

6-Month CliffEligible staking-related allocations remain locked during the initial six-month cliff.
24-Month VestingAfter the cliff, eligible allocations are intended to vest progressively over a 24-month period.
PurposeThe structure is intended to align token circulation with the continued development and adoption of marketplace utility.

8. Team & Contributor Vesting

The team and contributor allocation is subject to a long-term vesting structure intended to align contributor participation with sustained development.

12-Month CliffNo team or contributor allocation is intended to unlock during the initial twelve-month cliff.
24-Month VestingFollowing the cliff, the allocation is intended to vest progressively over 24 months.
36-Month Total ScheduleThe intended team/contributor schedule therefore consists of a 12-month cliff followed by 24 months of vesting.

9. Why Utility Is the Core Design

  • Creators have a direct reason to acquire and spend HDFI through eligible marketplace services.
  • Storefront owners can use HDFI for premium presentation and verification functionality.
  • Marketplace services create product-level use cases independent of speculative trading.
  • Vesting is intended to manage the pace at which allocated tokens enter circulation.
  • Future secondary-market liquidity is treated separately from the marketplace's core utility layer.

12. Conclusion

HDFI is designed as a utility token for the HedraFi marketplace, with its initial product role centered on premium creator and storefront functionality.

The architecture combines a Mainnet acquisition mechanism, defined token allocation, long-term vesting and marketplace utility. The design deliberately distinguishes primary token acquisition from future secondary-market liquidity while keeping the token's practical purpose connected to HedraFi's actual product.

The objective is straightforward: make HDFI useful inside the HedraFi marketplace and build the token economy around real product participation.
