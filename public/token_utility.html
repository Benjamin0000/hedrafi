Executive Overview

HedraFi has already established a live token economy on Hedera MainNet through its HBAR staking and HRT reward mechanism. More than 145,546,422 HRT has been distributed to participating users through real on-chain activity.

The next stage of HedraFi's evolution is the introduction of its official ecosystem token as the economic layer connecting the marketplace, creators, users and future HedraFi services.

Marketplace utility provides the token's initial direct use case. Users and creators will use the token to access premium marketplace functionality, including collection promotion, verified storefronts and enhanced storefront presentation.

Core principle: The token is designed around real product utility. Its initial demand is connected directly to marketplace participation rather than depending solely on secondary-market speculation.

How the Token Is Used

The intended interaction is simple and directly connected to marketplace functionality. A user selects a premium feature, reviews the required token amount, approves the transaction and receives the selected feature after successful payment.

Acquire Token → Connect Wallet → Select Feature → Approve & Pay → Feature Activated

Initial Economic Reference Price

HedraFi uses an initial economic reference price of $0.001 per token for initial economic modeling and marketplace utility pricing.

At a maximum supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens, a $0.001 reference price corresponds to an approximately $1,000,000 fully diluted valuation (FDV).

The reference price is an initial economic and utility-pricing reference. It is not presented as a guaranteed market price, future valuation or investment return.

Staking Reward Migration & Vesting

HedraFi's existing staking program has already distributed 145,546,422 HRT to participating users. As the ecosystem transitions toward the official HedraFi token, eligible staking-related allocations are designed to enter circulation through a controlled vesting mechanism.

6-Month Cliff Eligible staking-reward allocations remain locked during the initial six-month cliff period.
24-Month Vesting Following the cliff, eligible staking-reward allocations vest progressively over a 24-month period.
Purpose The structure is intended to align token circulation with the development of genuine marketplace utility and ecosystem demand.

Team & Contributor Vesting

The team and contributor allocation is subject to a longer vesting schedule to align contributor incentives with the long-term development of HedraFi.

12-Month Cliff No team or contributor allocation is intended to unlock during the initial twelve-month cliff.
36-Month Linear Vesting Following the cliff, the allocation is intended to vest gradually over 36 months.
48-Month Total Schedule The full team/contributor schedule therefore spans four years from the beginning of the vesting program.

This long-term structure is intended to align the interests of the development team and contributors with sustained ecosystem growth.

Why Marketplace Utility Comes First

HedraFi is intentionally establishing direct product utility before unrestricted secondary-market liquidity. This allows token demand to develop from actual marketplace usage while the ecosystem expands.

  • Creators have a reason to acquire and spend the token.
  • Store owners have a reason to use the token for premium functionality.
  • Marketplace activity creates recurring utility rather than one-time token distribution.
  • Controlled vesting reduces the risk of premature unrestricted circulation.
  • Future liquidity can be introduced against a marketplace with established users, functionality and economic activity.

Conclusion

The official HedraFi token is designed as the economic layer of the HedraFi ecosystem, with marketplace utility providing immediate practical functionality for creators and store owners.

By connecting token usage to real marketplace services, maintaining controlled circulation through vesting, and building on an existing MainNet community and staking economy, HedraFi is establishing a path toward sustainable ecosystem growth and deeper token utility.

HedraFi's objective is not simply to launch another token. It is to make the token an integral part of how creators, users and the marketplace participate in the HedraFi ecosystem.
