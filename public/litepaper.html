01 / Overview

A practical marketplace layer for the Hedera ecosystem

HedraFi aims to connect digital asset creators and collectors through a streamlined marketplace experience, with premium tools that help creators present their work and compete for attention.

Digital asset marketplaces can be difficult to navigate, and creator promotion tools are often disconnected from the places where collections are discovered. HedraFi’s product direction is to bring discovery, storefront presentation, and selected premium features into one accessible experience built around Hedera’s token and smart-contract infrastructure.

Our approach: prioritize a usable product, transparent on-chain interactions, and token utility tied to marketplace services. The long-term opportunity depends on execution, creator adoption, repeat usage, and responsible ecosystem development.

This litepaper summarizes HedraFi’s product direction and intended economic architecture. It is not a guarantee that every described feature is currently available, nor a promise of token value or investment return.

05 / Token Acquisition

Primary acquisition and secondary liquidity

HedraFi has described a buy-only HDFI bonding curve for primary token acquisition. Under this model, eligible users send HBAR to the curve contract and receive HDFI according to the contract’s pricing logic and available token balance.

Primary acquisition

Buy-only bonding curve

The curve is designed for purchases. It does not provide an on-contract sell-back or redemption function. Purchases are subject to the deployed contract’s actual parameters, balances, limits, and network conditions.

Separate mechanism

Secondary-market liquidity

Any future decentralized-exchange pool is a separate mechanism and depends on actual liquidity provision, market conditions, and the relevant protocol.

Before interacting, users should verify the contract address, token identity, price parameters, transaction details, and current contract state using official project links and a trusted Hedera explorer.

07 / Distribution & Vesting

Responsible circulation over time

Vesting is intended to align selected allocations with longer-term product development and reduce the likelihood that all allocated tokens become transferable at once. The following terms are proposed project parameters and should be treated as binding only where supported by final published terms and enforceable on-chain or contractual arrangements.

Staking and participation allocation

The project has discussed a six-month cliff followed by a 24-month vesting period for eligible allocations. Eligibility, quantities, commencement date, and implementation must be published before users rely on these terms.

Team and contributors

The project has discussed a 12-month cliff followed by gradual vesting. The final schedule, including the vesting duration and any contract enforcement, should be confirmed in the official token allocation disclosure.

Other allocations

Ecosystem, community, treasury, and liquidity allocations may be released or deployed under separate policies. Their actual status should be disclosed with wallet addresses, unlock schedules, or relevant on-chain references where available.

This document does not itself create a vesting contract, migration right, token claim, or governance entitlement.

09 / Roadmap

From marketplace foundation to a broader creator economy

HedraFi’s roadmap is organized around product capability and measurable adoption rather than token price targets.

Phase 1 · Core infrastructure

Maintain the Hedera-based token and contract foundation, improve monitoring, and publish useful on-chain references and adoption metrics.

Phase 2 · Marketplace utility

Deliver and refine core marketplace workflows, then activate premium features as implementation, testing, and operational readiness allow.

Phase 3 · Creator and community growth

Improve creator onboarding, collection discovery, community programs, and transparent reporting on holders and transaction activity.

Phase 4 · Liquidity and ecosystem expansion

Evaluate secondary-market liquidity and additional digital-asset use cases as the product matures. Any real-world asset (RWA) initiatives would require separate legal, compliance, technical, and operational assessment.

Roadmap items are objectives, not guarantees. Timing and scope may change based on engineering, security, adoption, funding, and regulatory considerations.

10 / Risks & Disclosures

Important considerations

Participation in blockchain networks and digital-asset ecosystems involves significant uncertainty. This litepaper is informational and does not constitute an offer to sell securities, investment advice, financial advice, or a guarantee of future performance.

  • Market risk: token prices, liquidity, and demand may fluctuate materially or may not develop.
  • Technology risk: smart contracts, wallets, network services, and third-party protocols can fail or be exploited.
  • Liquidity risk: a buy-only bonding curve is not a redemption mechanism; a secondary market may be unavailable or illiquid.
  • Delivery risk: planned features and roadmap milestones may be delayed, changed, or discontinued.
  • Tokenomics risk: allocations and proposed vesting terms should be checked against final implementation and public on-chain data.
  • Regulatory and tax risk: laws vary by jurisdiction and may change. Users are responsible for assessing their own obligations.

Never participate based solely on promotional materials. Verify token and contract addresses, understand transaction mechanics, and only use funds you can afford to lose.
